The service

What we handle

One clear point of contact from the first assessment through the available reporting process.

01 / ASSESS

We identify the actual issue

We read the review in context, check the right platform’s guidance and tell you which details matter. A low rating by itself is not a policy issue.

02 / PREPARE

We organize the evidence

We build a focused account of the concern using the review link, relevant dates and any records you can safely share. We do not ask for a password just to provide a quote.

03 / FOLLOW THROUGH

We manage the next steps

We use the available reporting route, explain meaningful updates and help you respond if the platform requests more information.

Working standards

Specific facts make a stronger report.

We will ask for evidence that relates to a platform rule, not a broad claim that a review is unfair. We keep the original post and your account of events separate, so the report stays clear and accurate.
