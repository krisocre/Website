Start with the review you want handled

Tell ReviewRemoval which business location is affected and how many reviews you want assessed. If you have the public link, add it. A short note about what happened helps us pick up the case. For several locations, list the city or branch alongside each review.

Your initial assessment is free. We use the public review and the details you choose to share, so your Google password stays with you.

See the work and fees before you approve

We’ll tell you which reviews are in scope, what evidence would help and which reporting and follow-up steps we can handle. You’ll see the CAD fees before approving. ReviewRemoval charges $50 upfront per approved review and $125 for each review successfully removed.

See the totals for a request with mixed outcomes.

Already reported the review? Pick up from there

Include the latest status or decision in your request if you have it. We’ll look at what was already submitted and what can support an available next step. For an eligible appeal, we can help prepare the review link, the original decision and the facts that relate to the policy concern.

Keep one point of contact through follow-up

Once you approve, we organise the facts for each review, prepare the report and handle the follow-up in your written scope. We’ll tell you when we need information, when there is a meaningful update and how each review is recorded for billing. For steps inside your own Business Profile, we’ll explain what you need to do.
