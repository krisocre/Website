Good to know

Questions about Trustpilot?

Practical answers before you hand us the review.

Can a business flag a Trustpilot review?+

Yes. Trustpilot provides flagging tools in its business account for reviews that may break its guidelines.

What if Trustpilot asks for more detail?+

We help organize the relevant facts and supporting documents, then explain what needs to be provided through the available process.

Does a low star rating qualify for removal?+

A rating alone is not a guideline issue. We assess the content and context of the specific review.

When is the $125 success fee charged?+

Only after a specific review is removed. The fee is charged separately for each successful removal.
