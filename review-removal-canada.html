Canadian review context

What Canada’s Competition Bureau says about review integrity.

The Bureau’s published guidance says businesses should not write or commission fake reviews or prevent people from seeing genuine, relevant and lawful reviews. Our work starts with a specific concern under the review platform’s rules. Criticism alone is not a reason to suppress a genuine customer account.

Source: Competition Bureau Canada, Deceptive Marketing Practices Digest, Volume 3. This is context from public guidance, not an assessment of any legal claim.

01

Keep genuine feedback visible. A negative experience may be useful to future customers and does not automatically violate a platform policy.

02

Report a specific rule concern. Preserve the review, identify the relevant policy and provide facts the platform can check.

03

Avoid rating manipulation. Do not commission fake positive reviews or offer a benefit in exchange for editing a critical one.

Common questions

Review removal questions from Canadian businesses

Platform policies apply to the review itself. Your city helps us identify the right listing and keep the request organized.

Is the service limited to Toronto or Ontario?+

No. We accept online requests from businesses in every Canadian province and territory. Share the public listing and review URL for the exact location.

Is pricing different in Vancouver, Montréal or Halifax?+

No. Our published rate is CAD $50 upfront per review and CAD $125 for each review removed, regardless of the Canadian city. We confirm applicable taxes and written terms before payment.

Does Canada have a separate Google review removal rule?+

Google evaluates reports against its platform policies. Being in Canada does not, by itself, make a review eligible for removal. See Google’s current reporting guidance.

What if our company has several Canadian locations?+

Send the exact review and business listing for each location. That lets us separate the cases and calculate the per-review fees correctly.

Do you need our account password?+

No password is needed for a quote. If a platform later requires a step from your business account, we explain that step rather than asking you to send a password through the form.
