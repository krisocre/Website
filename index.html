The review details

What goes into a Google review removal request?

We look for a specific issue in the review and organize the facts around it. Google’s published policies give the report its starting point.

01

Fake engagement

A review that may not reflect a genuine experience, or activity linked to paid or coordinated posting.

02

Conflicts of interest

Evidence that the reviewer has a relationship to the business, a competitor or another interested party.

03

Off-topic content

Material that is unrelated to an experience with the business or appears on the wrong profile.

04

Harmful or private content

Threats, harassment or personal information that may conflict with Google’s content rules.

Have a review in mind? Send the link and any context you can document. We’ll handle the assessment and reporting work.

Google review removal questions

Clear answers before you hand a review over to us. For platform rules and appeals, see our full review removal FAQ.

Can you help with a fake Google review in Toronto or elsewhere in Canada?+

Yes. Send the review and any relevant details. We assess whether it may breach Google’s fake engagement policy, prepare a focused report and manage the available follow-up. The same policy applies across Canada.

How long does a Google review removal request take?+

There is no fixed timeframe. It depends on Google’s review process and whether more information is requested. We keep you updated when there is a meaningful change.

What does the $50 upfront fee cover?+

It covers assessment, preparation and the managed removal work for one Google review. We confirm service terms before payment.

When is the $125 success fee charged?+

Only after the specific review is successfully removed. For multiple reviews, the fee applies only to those removed.

Do I need to give you my Google password?+

No password is needed to request a quote. If a later step requires action from your business account, we'll explain what to do.

Do you serve businesses outside Toronto?+

Yes. Our team accepts review requests from businesses across Ontario and Canada. The quote and reporting process is handled online.

What if I have several reviews?+

Choose the number above to see the full quote. The upfront fee is $50 for each review, and the $125 success fee applies separately to each one removed.
