Before you enquire

A few useful answers.

Find more platform and reporting guidance in our review removal FAQ.

Which Google reviews can you help with?+

We assess reviews that may violate Google’s policies, including suspected fake engagement, conflicts of interest, off-topic content or harmful material. Genuine negative feedback may remain online. If a review has disappeared, our missing-review guide explains a different issue.

Is an assessment request free?+

Yes. Sending your details costs nothing and authorizes no payment. For accepted paid work, the fee is CAD $50 upfront per review plus $125 for each review successfully removed. We confirm the scope, terms and applicable taxes before payment.

What if only some reviews are removed?+

The upfront fee is $50 for each review you approve us to handle. The $125 success fee applies only to each review removed. For three reviews with two successful removals, the total is $400 CAD before applicable tax. See our pricing guide for other outcomes.

How long does the process take?+

There is no guaranteed completion date. Google’s reporting and appeal process determines the timing. We explain meaningful updates and any available next step.

Do I need to share my Google password?+

No Google password is required for an initial assessment. Send public links only. If a later reporting step requires your business account, we explain the action for you to take yourself.
