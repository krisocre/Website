## Source basis

- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials) and [helpful content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): useful, reliable content and honest sourcing.
- [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies): avoid city pages that are substantially similar and funnel visitors to the same destination.
- [Google organization markup guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization): use real business details, not invented contact or address fields.
- [ISED tourism SME profile](https://www.ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/canadian-tourism-sector/sme-profile-2023-tourism-industries-canada): 2023 Canadian Google Reviews figures used for the new analysis.
- [Competition Bureau Canada, Deceptive Marketing Practices Digest, Volume 3](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices-digest-volume-3): Canadian context on fake reviews and suppression of genuine feedback.
