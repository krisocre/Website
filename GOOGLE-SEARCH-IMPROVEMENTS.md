# Google review removal improvements — October 13, 2026

## What the comparison establishes

The homepage already targeted Google review removal and the live apex/WWW,
canonical, robots and sitemap checks were successful. A different mix of query
impressions does not establish a crawl block or a Google penalty.

ReviewsBoost has more dedicated Google help pages. Its supplied Search Console
export shows searches for removal cost, reporting requests, report status and
missing reviews. These topics informed the new resources. The supplied export
is for ReviewsBoost, not ReviewRemoval; private export analysis stays in
`.seo-private/`, excluded from Git. Query exports omit some queries, and separate
country/query/page tables cannot establish a joined Canada-by-query measurement.

## Changes

1. Homepage title, heading and short lead identify Google review removal in Canada.
   Pricing stays second, the quote stays third, and CAD $50 + $125 stays unchanged.
2. Five distinct resources: cost and partial-success arithmetic; fake-review
   evidence; reporting and appeal status; missing-review diagnostics; a free
   concern-and-stage evidence worksheet with a text download.
3. Descriptive Google service navigation, contextual return links and footer links
   connect the service, research, Canada coverage and new resources. Every new
   resource has a direct homepage link. Other platform pages remain available.
4. Canada coverage names the Google service in the page heading and useful regional
   sections. Cities describe remote service coverage, not physical offices.
5. Accurate Article/BlogPosting/Breadcrumb and free-tool WebApplication markup;
   homepage service fees in CAD; refreshed collection metadata and sitemap.
6. The deployment script includes all five new pages and the original Google Ads
   base tag. A duplicate brand in the platform directory title was corrected.
7. The SEO audit checks every indexable page against the sitemap, internal files
   and fragments, duplicate IDs and homepage links to the Google resources.

The new copy and tool are original to ReviewRemoval. They are not copied from
ReviewsBoost. No removal success rate, client result, review count, office address
or rating was invented. No duplicate city landing pages were created.

## Publish and measure

The files need to be deployed before search engines can crawl these changes.
After deployment, inspect the homepage and the five new URLs, submit the sitemap
in Search Console and verify Google-selected canonicals. Use a consistent country
filter, search type and date window when comparing sites.

Track these separately:

- Google removal service queries: removal, fake-review removal and service pricing.
- Google informational queries: reporting, appeal status and missing reviews.
- Trustpilot queries and total site impressions.
- Clicks, CTR, landing pages and qualified quote enquiries.

Use equal-length windows, noting the deployment date. Do not infer that deleting
Trustpilot content reallocates impressions to Google, or that a higher Google
percentage proves an increase in Google impressions. Search Console access and
earned recognition remain separate from local code changes. There is no promise
that these changes will match another site's traffic.
