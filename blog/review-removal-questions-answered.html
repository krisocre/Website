Common review removal questions, answered

Fourteen direct answers about Google, Facebook, Yelp, Trustpilot, Tripadvisor and Glassdoor reporting, timelines, appeals and pricing.

By ReviewRemovalSeptember 21, 20267 min readSources checked September 2026

A bad review and a removable review are not the same thing. Platforms generally assess reports against their own rules. These answers explain the difference and link to the source behind each platform-specific point.

Eligibility and control

1. Can a business delete a customer review itself?

Usually the business can report or flag the review, but the platform evaluates it. Google says policy-violating reviews can be reported for removal; Trustpilot says reviewers own their own reviews and can edit or delete them. A service like ours can assess, prepare and manage the process, but cannot force a platform decision. Google Business Profile Help · Trustpilot business guidelines

2. Is a negative review enough to request removal?

No. Google says not to report a review merely because you dislike or disagree with it. Trustpilot says the same about a low rating or criticism. Look for a specific rule the content may violate, then provide relevant facts. Google reporting guidance · Trustpilot guidelines

3. What if the reviewer was never a customer?

Google’s policy requires reviews to reflect genuine experiences. Yelp’s guidance focuses on the reviewer’s own consumer experience. However, a business’s inability to find a matching name in its records is not, on its own, proof that no interaction happened. Document what you can establish and avoid claiming certainty without evidence. Google Maps content policy · Yelp reporting guidance

4. What if the reviewer is a competitor or former employee?

Google’s rating-manipulation policy includes conflicts of interest such as current or former employment and industry competitors. Yelp lists apparent conflicts of interest among its main reporting reasons and asks for specific evidence. The relationship and evidence matter more than an unsupported suspicion. Google Maps policy · Yelp guidance

Google Business Profile reviews

5. Which Google reviews are eligible for removal?

Google says only reviews that violate its policies qualify. Its policy covers fake engagement, rating manipulation, off-topic content and other prohibited material. Critical feedback about a genuine experience may remain, even when a business disputes the account. Google reporting guidance · Google Maps content policy

6. How do I report a Google review?

From your Business Profile, open Read reviews, choose the review, select Report, choose a reason and submit. Google also offers a Reviews Management Tool to file and track reports. Interface labels may change, so use Google’s current help page for exact steps. Google’s step-by-step guide

7. How long does Google take to decide?

Google says review evaluation typically takes several days. That is guidance, not a deadline. The Reviews Management Tool can show whether a decision is pending, no violation was found, or an appeal was escalated. Google reporting guidance

8. Can I appeal if Google leaves the review up?

Google describes a one-time appeal for eligible reviews after a no-violation decision. Its current tool instructions allow selecting up to ten reviews for an appeal. Check the tool for your own profile and review status. Google appeal steps

Facebook, Yelp, Trustpilot, Tripadvisor and Glassdoor

9. Can I remove one Facebook recommendation by switching Recommendations off?

No. Meta says turning Recommendations off disables that feature and removes the Page’s rating and reviews from display as a whole. Meta also says you can report an individual Recommendation or review if it does not follow Community Standards. Meta Help Center

10. Does Yelp’s “not recommended” label mean the review was removed?

No. Yelp’s recommendation software and its content-moderation reports are separate. A review can be not currently recommended without being removed. Yelp says reporting a review does not guarantee removal. Yelp moderation guidance · Yelp reporting guidance

11. Can a business flag a Trustpilot review for being unfair?

Trustpilot says businesses may flag reviews that breach its guidelines, but a disliked star rating or disagreement with a negative account is not a valid reason. Its content integrity process makes the decision. Trustpilot guidelines

12. Can Tripadvisor remove a review posted to the wrong property?

Tripadvisor lists a review on the wrong property as one circumstance for submitting a concern. It directs registered owners to the Reviews area of the Management Center. Tripadvisor says no removal is guaranteed. Tripadvisor for Business

13. Can an employer flag a Glassdoor review?

Glassdoor says employers can use the flag icon beneath a review to request another moderation look. It assesses the content against its guidelines; disagreement alone need not warrant removal. Glassdoor guidance
